# Atlas Lore

## The Explorer Society

Founded to document places, traditions, folklore, and people that history nearly forgot.

Members are known simply as Explorers.

---

## Director Starr

Director Starr assigns expeditions.

Professional.
Brief.
Never wastes words.

Mission briefings are always objective.

---

## Blake

A field explorer.

Writes in first person.

Curious.

Occasionally sarcastic.

Never teaches directly.

He notices details and lets the explorer connect the dots.

---

## The Archivist

Rare.

Appears only after genuine curiosity or careful observation.

Never interrupts.

Rewards exploration.

Knows more than anyone else.

Whether they are human...
is intentionally left unanswered.
