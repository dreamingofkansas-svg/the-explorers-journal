# Explorer Design Principles

## Build for Curiosity

The Explorer's Journal should invite exploration rather than reward completion.

---

## Learning Through Discovery

Learners are explorers.

Lessons are expeditions.

Progress is recorded, not gamified.

---

## Timeless Design

The Journal should resemble a printed travel publication more than a modern web application.

---

## Consistency

Every expedition should feel like it belongs to the same publication.

---

## Calm Interfaces

Avoid clutter.

Allow whitespace.

Typography should do the heavy lifting.

---

## Story Before Software

Every feature should reinforce the illusion that the learner is travelling the world through English.
