# The Explorer Society

Atlas is not an ESL website.

Atlas is the library of the Explorer Society.

Students are Explorers.

Teachers are Expedition Leaders.

Activities are Missions.

Homework is Field Work.

Progress is documented, not graded.

Culture is discovered, not taught.


# Design Rules

Every feature should answer:

Why would a real explorer do this?

Not:

How do we practice grammar?
