# Mission Statement

Explore the traditions, food, sounds, and rituals of an American baseball game.

Students should leave feeling like they attended a game, not studied one.

---

# Explorer Experience

Emotion:
Excitement

Core Question:

"What makes Game Day feel special?"

Rather than teaching baseball rules, this guide explores the culture surrounding the game.

---

# Learning Goals

Students will...

• Compare traditions

• Express preferences

• Interview classmates

• Explain opinions

• Recommend experiences

• Tell short stories

• Ask follow-up questions

Grammar is embedded naturally through conversation.
